(01) Batches

A few things out of the oven,
and a few still rising.

  1. 01NativeDiagram AI makes editable Office SmartArt, delivered as .docx or .pptx over MCP. Listed in Claude’s connector directory. Served↗
  2. 02Liveleaf Open AI-made HTML files on your iPhone, offline. Pages that just work, without a server. Cooling — soon↗
  3. 03OwnFlick Self-hosted player for your local videos, images and music. Swipe your own library like a feed, or leave it on as radio. Served↗
  4. 04QRHop A Mac menu-bar tool: press a shortcut and your clipboard becomes a QR code, ready for your phone’s camera. Cooling — soon↗
